THE GAP

Parents finance education. Visibility shouldn't be a guessing game.

Involved creates a structured channel between institutions and authorized sponsors, reducing information gaps around academic progress, registration and fees.

01

Visibility gap

Sponsors can be responsible for tuition while having limited structured visibility into the student's academic journey.

02

Communication overload

Universities can centralize common sponsor questions through a controlled digital experience.

03

Accountability

Verified information helps families make earlier, better-informed decisions about fees and academic support.
